    Two-dimensional tissues¶

    The package-level functions (infer_tensions, infer_tensions_by_junction_component, infer_pressures, infer_forces, infer_tensions_then_pressures, dcel_mesh_from_segmentation_mask, dcel_mesh_from_file) take 2-D meshes and masks: their pages are those of the 3-D functions (tension inference, pressure inference, force inference, input and output). This page lists the names that exist only in 2D. Provisional: they may change in any release until they are declared stable. The user guide's page on two-dimensional tissues explains them.

    The 2-D mesh¶

    foambryo's 2-D mesh, PlanarMesh, and the rule that tells a 2-D mesh from a 3-D one.

    foambryo's package-level functions (infer_tensions, infer_pressures, infer_forces, ...) take a 3-D mesh (foambryo.dcel.DcelData, triangles) or a 2-D one (PlanarMesh, segments) and dispatch on its type (dimension_of). A 2-D mesh is the one delaunay-watershed's 2-D reconstruction (dw2d) builds:

    • points (n, 2) float64, in the mask's index order (row, column), in pixels (or in the unit of an isotropic pixel size);
    • segments (m, 2) int64, the two vertex indices of each edge;
    • labels (m, 2) int64, the two materials each edge separates, smaller first (0 is the medium).

    Orientation: one rule in 2D and 3D, in index order. Each mesh element is the positively oriented boundary of its first label: a triangle's right-hand normal (p1 - p0) x (p2 - p0) in (z, y, x) points out of labels[k, 0], and material labels[k, 0] lies on the left of the edge segments[k, 0] -> segments[k, 1] in (row, column), so that the normal (dc, -dr) of an edge of tangent (dr, dc) points out of labels[k, 0] too. dw2d follows it since 0.8.0; dw2d 0.1.5 and the development versions of 0.8.0 stored every edge the other way round. The 2-D pressures rely on it (which side of an interface each cell is on).

    foambryo does not trust a 2-D mesh's orientation, it measures it: on input (PlanarMesh, a mesh file, dw2d's DCEL_Data or arrays given to any 2-D function), the signed area of each cell's boundary, A = (1/2) sum (r0 c1 - c0 r1) over its edges (each counted as stored where the cell is the first label, reversed where it is the second), is positive for every cell under the one rule and negative for every cell under the former one. A mesh in the former orientation has every edge reversed, so the results are the same from either; a mesh whose cells disagree is refused (ValueError). Only cells with a closed boundary vote, and the cells cut by the image border only when no other cell can (dw2d 0.1.5 left its image-border edges in an arbitrary order: a cut cell then votes with its area taken along its own loops, each image-border edge in the direction its loop runs); a mesh with no closed cell is taken as given.

    dw2d's own DCEL_Data, any object with v (n, 2) and e (m, 4) (e = segments and labels side by side), a (points, segments, labels) triple and a (vertices, edges) pair are accepted too, and read the same way.

    foambryo.image_border_vertices ¶

    image_border_vertices(mesh: object) -> set[int]

    Vertices of a 2D mesh that lie on the image border: dw2d's mark, or else the vertex positions.

    Where a junction lies on the image border, the border cuts a cell-cell interface and dw2d's medium edge along the border makes a straight 180-degree wedge that is not a contact angle.

    • dw2d's mark (a mesh with an image_border_vertices attribute that is not None: dw2d 0.8.0's DCEL_Data, from geometry_reconstruction_2d(...).return_dcel()): those vertices, which dw2d takes from its triangulation's structure (the endpoints of its hull edges) before any vertex moves, and which its junction relocation never moves.
    • Otherwise, the positions (a (vertices, edges) pair, or an object without the mark): dw2d adds the four image corners to its Delaunay points and keeps them as mesh vertices, so the image rectangle is the bounding box of the vertices and the border vertices are those on its sides. Exact on a mesh whose border vertices did not move. If the four corners of the bounding box are not vertices (a mesh that did not come from dw2d), the border cannot be located: a UserWarning is raised and no vertex is returned. To exclude the border of a relocated mesh given as arrays, pass dw2d's mark as excluded_vertices (with exclude_image_border=False).

    foambryo.IllConditionedLensWarning ¶

    Bases: UserWarning

    Interfaces whose rows come from the same junctions only were refused: the angles cannot separate their tensions.

    Their tensions are nan and those junctions give no row (module docstring, "Lenses"). The usual cause is a small lens (a cell or a pocket of medium with only two sides) whose two sides are read by their chords, which coincide. The report's refused_lenses lists each group, its junction vertices and its condition number.

    foambryo.NoJunctionError ¶

    NoJunctionError(interfaces: object)

    Bases: UndeterminedInferenceError

    The 2D mesh has no junction, so no tension can be inferred: its cells never meet.

    A tension is read from the angles at a junction, a vertex where three or more regions meet. On a label mask whose cells are separated from one another by background pixels (a common output of segmentation tools), every interface is a cell-medium loop, no junction exists, and every tension would be undetermined. Such a result carries no value, so the inference refuses it. The remedy is to grow the labels until neighbouring cells meet, with dw2d's expansion_labels argument, geometry_reconstruction_2d(labels, expansion_labels=...) (a distance in pixels).

    Fires only when the mesh has no vertex where three or more regions meet; a mesh whose interfaces are only partly undetermined is not refused (those interfaces are nan).

    Attributes:

    Name Type Description
    interfaces

    the interfaces of the mesh (sorted material pairs), every one undetermined.

    Build the error and its message from the interfaces of the mesh.

    The global fit (opt-in)¶

    foambryo.infer_forces_by_equilibrium_tiling ¶

    infer_forces_by_equilibrium_tiling(mesh: object, labels: NDArray[integer], mean_tension: float = 1.0, *, max_evaluations: int = EQUILIBRIUM_TILING_MAX_EVALUATIONS, diagnostics: bool = False) -> tuple[ComponentTensionSolution, dict[int, float]] | tuple[ComponentTensionSolution, dict[int, float], EquilibriumTilingReport]

    Tensions and pressures by a global fit of an equilibrium tiling of circular arcs (provisional, opt-in).

    The label image's crack points (the middle of every side shared by two 4-adjacent pixels of different labels) are fitted by a Möbius tiling (Noll N. et al., Phys. Rev. X 10, 011072, 2020, doi:10.1103/PhysRevX.10.011072): every material carries f(x) = A |x|^2 + B . x + C, every interface is the circle where two f are equal, Young's and Laplace's laws hold exactly, and the fit minimises the squared distance of the points to their circles (Levenberg-Marquardt, sparse analytic Jacobian). Start: Noll's algebraic problem with each interface's residual divided by the tension of foambryo's arc reading of mesh (infer_tensions_by_junction_component at its defaults) and one anchor per interface, so that the start is measured in pixels; this is what makes large tissues and crops converge to the true tiling. Tensions share a scale only within a block (biconnected component) of the graph of materials linked by interfaces: each block is fitted alone and gauged to mean(tension) = mean_tension.

    Named after what it returns, tensions and pressures in one call, like foambryo.infer_forces; the mesh comes first, as in every infer_* function (infer_by_arc_tiling(labels, mesh) before 0.8.0, never released).

    mesh object

    		 required
    labels NDArray[integer]

    the label image ((rows, columns) integers, 0 the medium) that mesh was built from.

    		 required
    mean_tension float

    each block's mean tension.

    		 1.0
    max_evaluations int

    the fit's largest number of residual evaluations.

    		 EQUILIBRIUM_TILING_MAX_EVALUATIONS
    diagnostics bool

    also return an EquilibriumTilingReport.

    		 False

    when labels is not the image mesh was built from, as far as it can be checked: not 2-D, a shape other than the mesh's image_shape (when the mesh records it), or a cell of the mesh absent from labels.

    Refusals (local, never the whole image): an interface with fewer than 3 crack points; an interface that is a block by itself; an interface of a cell whose fitted pressure is not above the medium's (an unbounded region), or that is empty in the fitted tiling; an interface of the data that the fitted tiling does not have in the image.

    Measured on synthetic equilibrium foams with known tensions and pressures (307 held-out tissues of 4 to 64 cells, exact masks, crops, smooth boundary noise of 0.5 to 2 px, the 0.75 px measured on real 2D masks, Cellpose segmentations of rendered images, and images whose membranes are displaced 0.75 px off equilibrium; cells of 10 to 40 px radius), against the arc reading of infer_tensions_by_junction_component: the median relative tension error is lower in every tier and scale, by 0.03 to 0.11 (95 % intervals below 0), except at 2 px of noise and 20 px radius (-0.005, interval -0.014 to +0.005); on a 4 096-cell tissue (10 px radius) 0.077 against 0.156, every interface kept, in 74 s and 0.6 GiB. It refuses whole images where the arc reading refuses none: 11 of 453 crops (the fit slides along the one direction of the cost that a cut tissue barely determines, the ratio of the cell-medium to the cell-cell tensions), so it is offered beside the arc reading, not as the default.
