The `least_squares` and `median_one` gauges are in the per-case JSON and in `summary.json`; `median_one` is the one that can see a factor applied to a minority of interfaces. See `benchmarks/metrics.effective_tension_ratios`.

`res interior` is the fraction of the squared residual carried by interface-interior vertices. High means the interfaces themselves are not at equilibrium (not converged); low means the residual sits at the triple lines, which is expected discretisation error.
