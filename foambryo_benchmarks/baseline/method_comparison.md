# Tension-inference method comparison, by equilibrium group
Generated by `benchmarks/run_baseline.py`. Every number is the **median over cases** of that case's **median relative tension error** against the ground-truth tensions, on the ground-truth `.rec` mesh (no `dw3d` involved).

**The two groups are never combined** — the recorded decision excluded the five non-equilibrium cases from every accuracy claim and kept them as a negative control. See `benchmarks/case_groups.py` for the decision and the three measurements behind it.

**Gauge** (the rule every accuracy comparison in this package follows): errors are given as published *and* after matching the overall tension scale. Only tension *ratios* are physical, so a method whose soft normalisation row does not hold can look far worse than it is — which the `max |1 - mean(g)|` column exposes.
