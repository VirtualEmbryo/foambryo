# Uncertainty calibration — `error/std` for every variant

Generated by `benchmarks/run_uncertainty_calibration.py` (the uncertainty work). `error/std` is the median over interfaces of `|inferred - truth| / std`, **gauge-matched** (the interval is rescaled by the same factor as the tensions). **1 is calibrated**; above 1 the interval is optimistic (dangerous), below 1 conservative (safe, less useful).

The two equilibrium groups are never combined: by recorded decision, the five non-equilibrium cases are excluded from every accuracy claim.
